    ************
    Nomenclature
    ************

.. glossary::
   :sorted:

   ACS
      Attitude Control System.

   ADC
      Analog-to-Digital Converter.

   AIT
      Assembly, Integration and Testing.

   ANATEL
      *Agência Nacional de Telecomunicações*, or Brazilian National Telecommunications Agency.

   AR
      Acceptance Review.

   BOL
      Beginning Of Life.

   CDR
      Critical Design Review.

   CDS
      CubeSat Design Specification.

   CNPq
      *Conselho Nacional de Desenvolvimento Científico e Tecnológico*, or National Council for Scientific and Technological Development.

   COTS
      Commercial Off-The-Shelf.

   CV
      Constant Voltage.

   DCP
      Data Collection Platform.

   DoD
      Depth of Discharge.

   ECSS
      European Cooperation for Space Standardization.

   EDC
      Environmental Data Collector.

   EM
      Engineering Model.

   EMMN
      *Estação Multimissão de Natal*, or Natal Multi-Mission Station.

   EOL
      End Of Life.

   EPS
      Electrical Power System.

   EPS1
      Electrical Power System 1.0.

   EPS2
      Electrical Power System 2.0.

   FEC
      Forward Error Correction.

   FM
      Flight Model.

   FSPL
      Free-Space Path Loss.

   GMAT
      General Mission Analysis Tool.

   GMSK
      Gaussian Minimum Shift Keying.

   HMAC
      Hash-based Message Authentication Code.

   IC
      Integrated Circuit.

   ID
      Identification Data.

   IIP
      Interstage Interface Panels.

   INPE
      *Instituto Nacional de Pesquisas Espaciais*, or National Institute for Space Research.

   LEO
      Low Earth Orbit.

   LIT
      *Laboratório de Integração e Testes*, or Integration and Testing Laboratory (INPE).

   LNA
      Low Noise Amplifier.

   MCU
      Microcontroller Unit.

   MDR
      Mission Definition Review.

   MPP
      Maximum Power Point.

   MPPT
      Maximum Power Point Tracking.

   OBC
      On-Board Computer.

   OBDH
      On-Board Data Handling.

   P&O
      Perturb and Observe.

   PA
      Power Amplifier.

   PCB
      Printed Circuit Board.

   PDR
      Preliminary Design Review.

   PTT
      Platform Transmitter Terminal.

   PV
      Photovoltaic.

   PWM
      Pulse-Width Modulation.

   RBF
      Remove Before Flight.

   RE²PS
      Reliability Enhanced Electrical Power System.

   RTD
      Resistance Temperature Detector.

   SBCD
      *Sistema Brasileiro de Coleta de Dados*, or Brazilian Environmental Data Collection System.

   SDR
      Software Defined Radio.

   SEE
      Single Event Effects.

   SHA-1
      Secure Hash Algorithm 1.

   SLCAM
      SpaceLab Camera.

   SNR
      Signal-to-Noise Ratio.

   SPI
      Serial Peripheral Interface.

   TBC
      To Be Confirmed.

   TBD
      To Be Defined.

   TID
      Total Ionizing Dose.

   TMTC
      Telemetry and Telecommand.

   TP
      Test Plan.

   TRL
      Technology Readiness Level.

   TTC
      Telemetry, Tracking and Command.

   UHF
      Ultra High Frequency.

   VHF
      Very High Frequency.

   VP
      Verification Plan.

   WBS
      Work Breakdown Structure.

   WP
      Work Package.
